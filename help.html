Frequently asked questions

Who is this admission portal for?

It is exclusively for applicants to Ethan Digital Academy: students, professionals and business owners.

Has my application been submitted?

Complete the application, choose Submit via WhatsApp or Submit via Email, then press Send in the app that opens. Saving a draft or opening a message does not send it. Admissions will confirm receipt and advise on the next steps.

Can I continue on another device?

Drafts do not sync across devices. You can download a copy for your records and continue using the original browser.

Can children apply?

Applicants under 18 should prepare the application with a parent or guardian. The form requests guardian details when Under 18 is selected.

What are the fees and admission dates?

Contact the Academy for confirmed fees, intake dates, requirements and programme availability.

Can I upload documents or pay here?

Document uploads and live payments are not connected in this preview.
