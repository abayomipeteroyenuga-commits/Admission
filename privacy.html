YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy & application terms.

Local preview storage

Your saved draft stays in the current browser’s local storage. There is no applicant account or online database in this version. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to view the saved draft.

Your control

Download a copy or delete the local draft from My Application. Clearing browser storage may also remove your draft. Downloaded copies stay on your device until you delete them.

External contact links

WhatsApp and email links open external services. Application submission links include your entered details in a prepared message to the Academy. You choose WhatsApp or email, review the message and press Send. Your selected provider processes the message under its own terms. Sending shares the application, including guardian details where applicable, with Ethan Digital Academy. The website cannot confirm sending or receipt. Draft saving alone shares nothing.

Admission information

Programme examples are proposed learning paths. They do not confirm available places, fees, schedules or acceptance. Only Ethan Digital Academy can confirm admission and enrolment.

Before launch

A live portal will need an approved privacy notice covering server storage, document handling, access, retention and contact procedures.
